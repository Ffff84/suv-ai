**FAR-OLMA: метраж полива по насосу 60 м³/ч против базы, посчитанной по 24 м³/ч**

baseline_m3_per_ha=228.6 выведен из расхода КАПЕЛЬНОЙ системы 24 м³/ч (576 м³ за 24-часовой сеанс, см. _baseline), а bot/main.py _log_one считает actual_m3 = часы × pump.m3_per_hour = часы × 60. Фермер знает длительность сеанса («сеансы по 24 часа») и отвечает /bajardim 24 -> записано 1440 м³ вместо 576; воспроизведено: saved=-863.9 м³ за один сеанс по точной прежней норме. Кнопки часов (6-12) не совпадают ни с 24 ч сеанса, ни с 9.6 ч работы насоса, так что любой ответ через кнопки тоже искажает объём. База и метраж обязаны считаться по одному расходу, иначе KPI по саду всегда врёт.

*Поправка скептика:* The core is real, but the mechanism is a mis-answer/unverified-physics hazard, not a deterministic unit mismatch. (1) The bot explicitly asks pump hours, not session length («Nasos necha soat ishladi?», bot/main.py:740/766), and its recommendation message computes hours at the same 60 m³/h («включите насос на 10 ч» for 576 m³; pinned by tests/test_bughunt_20260817.py: 576/60=9.6 -> «10 soat»), so metering (hours*60) is internally consistent with what the bot asks and instructs; the 24 m³/ч figure lives only in comment keys («_полив», «_расход_капли») that no code reads. «KPI по саду всегда врёт» is therefore overstated: in the guided flow (farmer follows the rec and presses the matching button) the record is consistent within rounding. (2) The buttons claim is half wrong: HOUR_OPTIONS=(6,8,9,10,12) includes 10, which is exactly the rounded 9.6 pump-hours of an old-norm session (records 600 vs true 576, +4%), matching the rec's own rounding. The real defect stands as follows: olma.json carries two unreconciled flow rates whose relationship is explicitly unconfirmed in the config itself («вероятно через накопитель. Требует подтверждения», olma.json:32), while the farmer's own unit of thought is the 24-hour session (the _baseline note is written from his words). One natural typed answer /bajardim 24 records 1440 m³ (validation allows 0.5..48) and drives the season KPI to -863.9 m³, shown unclamped by /tejaldi; and if there is no reservoir (24 m³/ч is the true system throughput), every hours-based record overstates by 2.5x even with honest pump-hours answers. Aggravating factor missing from the finding: the wrong record is uncorrectable — LEDGER.has_action dedup (bot/main.py:649) rejects a corrected retry, and no code path updates or deletes actions. Fix direction: confirm the reservoir question, make base and metering share one confirmed delivered-volume model, and either cap/flag actual_m3 wildly above gross_m3 or allow correcting a logged action.

**root_depth() resets mature perennials to 0.20 m roots every spring, shrinking TAW/RAW ~3-7x**

For perennials, schedule.simulate computes dap from season_start(), which resets to this year's bud break (crop.py:105-112). root_depth() then restarts from z_min=0.20 m every spring, as if a 9-year-old orchard regrows its roots annually. Additionally, at bud break zr collapses 1.5->0.2 m mid-simulation and soil.step caps the carried depletion at the new tiny TAW (min(depletion, taw), soil.py:179), silently erasing up to ~220 mm of accumulated winter deficit; the following days the growth-dilution branch (soil.py:168-169) further scales what little remains. FAO-56 treats Zr of established trees/vines as constant at maximum.

*Поправка скептика:* Two scenario details need correction. First, the 'silently erasing up to ~220 mm of accumulated winter deficit' is a theoretical ceiling that real runs never approach: warm-start always begins at depletion 0 at most 92 days back (bot/main.py _rewind, run_field.warm_start), and winter ETc minus effective rain is small — under Samarkand climate normals the deficit accumulated by Mar 19 was only ~6 mm, so the practical erasure is tens of mm at most. Second, the real fields are apple planted 2018-03-20 on sandy_loam (fields/olma.json, drip, 25 mm net cap making the small-dose effect stronger) and grape planted 2017 (fields/uzum.json), not 'apple 2017 on loam'; on sandy_loam the spring numbers are TAW 59.8 mm vs mature 195 mm — the same ~3.3x shrink, so the conclusion is unchanged. **Synthetic rain generator delivers ~2x the monthly normal in wet months**

synth_day() adds rain on doy%9==0 (~3.3 events/month at monthly_rain/3.5 = 0.94x normal) AND on every day with wob>2.0 when monthly_rain>15 (sin(doy*2.399)>0.8 holds ~20.5% of days, ~6.1 days/month at monthly_rain/6 = 1.02x normal). This is the exact series bot/main.py uses in degraded mode (weather feed down: season(STATIONS['samarkand'],...) rewound from the last irrigation) — an extra ~80 mm of phantom effective rain over a spring rewind wipes the real deficit and the bot answers 'полив не требуется' for a field that is actually dry.

*Поправка скептика:* The ~2x generation math and the degraded-mode data path are exactly as stated, but the impact window is wrong. The real harm is (a) rewinds ending in late May-June, where the phantom rain from the spanned wet months understates today's depletion by up to ~26 mm and delays the first irrigation of the season by 1-5 days (grape: 'irrigate today' degrades to 'in 2 days'); (b) autumn rewinds (mid-November, long gap), where the flip to 'полив не требуется' (reason rain_expected, driven partly by phantom rain in the 14-day forecast half of the same series) does occur while the corrected series says irrigate today; and additionally the doubled rain in the forecast half inflates rain_ahead, biasing recommend() toward rain_expected/after_rain postponements. Summer-only outages (Jun-Aug) are unaffected because monthly normals under 15 mm disable the wob branch and the doy%9 events fall below the 3 mm effective-rainfall floor.
